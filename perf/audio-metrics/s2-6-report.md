Node baseline: the first play creates the whole audio graph once (created=85 on this run: 30 EQ biquads + masterGain + analyser + the effects-chain block from AudioEffectsManager.init + 1 MediaElementSource). The switch/seek burst created 0 additional nodes.

Write-rate context: the two playback keys are throttled per key (2s), so the combined rate sits exactly at the <=1/s boundary (0.5/s per key); S2-4's 0.4/s target is not met by the current throttle configuration. The all-key rate is higher because the first play also unlocks achievements (`stats-achievements-store-v4` burst) and writes journal/liner-notes/performance entries once.

## Blockers and gaps (recorded as-is)

- All scenarios were captured on this machine; no blockers.
- Plan-level scenarios `effects-drag` (100 intensity drags) and `soak-100-skip` (100 switches) plus the 2h soak (S7-9) are not covered here; this run covers idle / play / 5 switch+seek actions at 60s.
- The silent clip is 100s (just above the app's 95s trial-snippet threshold), so seek targets are 60%/25% of that clip; a real 3-minute track is not covered.
- Headless Chrome has no real sound card or GPU; rAF is compositor-driven, so writes/long-tasks/node counts are the primary signals and rAF numbers are a local baseline.
- Console errors are recorded, not fatal. In this dev environment they are 401 responses from `/api/ai/chat` (AI backend not running/authorized here), listed under `badResponses` in each JSON; they are not audio-path failures.
